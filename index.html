01 — Services

What we fix, install, and stand behind.

Repair and installation across all major kitchen and laundry appliances. If it plugs in, hooks up, or vents out, we probably handle it — ask if you're unsure.

Refrigerators & freezers

Cooling, leaks, ice makers, water lines, compressor diagnostics, and door alignment.

Ovens, ranges & stoves

Heating elements, igniters, control boards, gas valves, temperature calibration.

Microwaves & vent hoods

Magnetrons, door switches, fan motors, light replacements, vent re-routing.

Dishwashers

Drain pumps, spray arms, leaks, error codes, and full reinstalls.

Washers

Drain issues, agitator and motor faults, bearings, balance and vibration fixes.

Dryers

Heat issues, drum belts, vent cleaning, sensor faults, gas and electric.

Garbage disposals

Replacements, jams, leaks under the sink, switch and breaker diagnostics.

Wine coolers & ice makers

Temperature drift, fan and thermistor swaps, water line installs and repairs.
